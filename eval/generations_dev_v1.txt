Q: How much notice must I give if I want to resign?
A: You must give at least four weeks' notice if you have been employed for less than two years, six weeks' notice if you have been employed for two years or more but less than five years, or eight weeks' notice if you have been employed for five years or more on the date of your resignation. [s12(2)(a)]

q04 [answer] gold=['s60F']
Q: How many days of paid sick leave do I get?
A: The number of days of paid sick leave you get depends on the length of your employment with the same employer. 

For employees who have been employed for less than two years, you get 14 days in each calendar year. 
For employees who have been employed for two years or more but less than five years, you get 18 days in each calendar year. 
For employees who have been employed for five years or more, you get 22 days in each calendar year. 

[ s60F ]

A: No, your employer must give you 14 days' notice before terminating your contract, unless there is a wilful breach by you of a condition of the contract. [s57]
