q13 gold=['s60A'] retrieved=['s100', 's60A', 's60D'] cited=['s3F']
Q: How much more do I get paid for overtime?
A: For any overtime work carried out in excess of the normal hours of work, the employee shall be paid at a rate not less than one and half times his hourly rate of pay irrespective of the basis on which his rate of pay is fixed. [s3F]

q16 gold=['s60E'] retrieved=['s60E', 's100', 's60F'] cited=['s60F']
Q: What happens to my annual leave if I fall sick during it?
A: If an employee becomes entitled to sick leave while on paid annual leave, the employee shall be granted the sick leave, and the annual leave shall be deemed to have not been taken in respect of the days for which sick leave is so granted [s60F(1b)].

q22 gold=['s13'] retrieved=['s57', 's21', 's13'] cited=['s57']
Q: Can my employer pay me money instead of giving me notice when ending my contract?
A: No, your employer must give you 14 days' notice before terminating your contract, unless there is a wilful breach by you of a condition of the contract. [s57]

[Note: s57 states that a contract can be terminated without notice and without paying an indemnity on the ground of conduct by the other party inconsistent with the terms and conditions of the contract, but this is not the case for terminating a contract without notice.]
